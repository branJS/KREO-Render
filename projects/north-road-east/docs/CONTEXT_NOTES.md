# North Road East — Site & Context Notes

Findings from quick research into the actual building location.

## The building

- **Address:** 74 North Road East, Plymouth, PL4 6AL
- **Currently let by:** Student Choice Plymouth (`studentchoiceplymouth.com`)
- **Original developer (per Bailey Partnership drawings):** Hermes Great Estate Ltd / New Hermes Estate Ltd
- **The Bailey Partnership construction drawings Brandon supplied are the same building** — confirmed by the address and footprint match

## Plymouth context

- North Road East is a short residential street running roughly east-west, located between **Greenbank Road / North Hill** to the north and the **University of Plymouth campus** to the south
- Walking distance to: Plymouth University main campus (~3 min south), Plymouth railway station (~5 min west), Drake Circus shopping (~7 min south-west)
- Neighbouring fabric: a mix of Victorian/Edwardian red-brick terraces (typical Plymouth student rental stock), some interwar render buildings, and a few modern student-accommodation infill builds
- The road carries light traffic, has on-street parking, and is residential in character
- Postcode area PL4 is the inner-eastern residential belt of Plymouth — heavy student-tenant demographic given proximity to the university

## What this means for the cinematic film

- **The "drone establishing shot" backdrop should show:** modest mixed-period UK terraced rooftops to the north and south, with the larger institutional buildings of Plymouth University visible to the south horizon. Avoid generic London-style mansion blocks or US/EU contexts.
- **The "real photos" act of the three-act narrative:** Brandon can capture both his building AND the street context himself. Walking 50m in either direction gives him: north — towards Greenbank Road and traditional terraces; south — towards University of Plymouth campus.
- **Sun path for golden hour:** Plymouth at this latitude (50.37°N) means in early May, sunset is around 8:35 PM, golden hour starts ~7:50 PM. The street faces approximately south, so the sun sets to the **west (camera-left** if shooting the marketing-render hero composition). This matches the lighting direction we already specified in Shot 2.
- **Sun path for blue hour:** ~8:35 PM through to ~9:10 PM. The dusk marketing render's deep magenta-violet sky is consistent with this timing.
